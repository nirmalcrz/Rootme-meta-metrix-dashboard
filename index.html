How the numbers are counted
  • GST is added at 18% on top of the spend Meta reports.
  • Ad leads: for each campaign, form leads and chat conversations are compared and the higher one is counted.
  • Cost per result uses spend without GST.
  • Important leads are Hot, Warm, Slot Booked and Office Visit. Worst are Lost and Unfit.
  • Lead status is the status on the day the sheet was exported, not on the day the lead came in.
