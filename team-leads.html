How these are counted
  • Meta leads come from the Rootme Skills ad account. For each campaign, form leads and chat conversations are compared and the higher one is counted. The Rootme English ad account is not connected yet.
  • Metrix leads are leads in Metrix whose campaign is one of the Meta campaigns for that section. Organic leads are Metrix leads from the organic campaigns and sources for that section. A lead is counted once: if its campaign is a Meta campaign it is not counted as organic.
  • Good is Hot, Warm, Office Visit and Slot Booked. Bad is Unfit and Lost. No idea is every other status (New, Not Connected, Qualified, Cold, Online).
  • Workshop covers the Video Editing and AI workshops. "Not tagged" means the lead's campaign does not name a course.
  • Meta and Metrix counts will not match exactly: not every ad chat is entered in Metrix.
