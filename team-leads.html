Count per period and source

How these are counted
  • Meta ad leads are from the Rootme Skills ad account only. For each campaign, form leads and chat conversations are compared and the higher one is counted.
  • Metrix leads are every lead created in Metrix on that day, from any source. Rootme Skills means Video Editing, Contech Marketing, AI Web Development and Graphic Design, including their workshops. Other leads are English, other courses and leads with an empty Campaign field.
  • Organic Rootme Skills leads are Metrix leads from these sources: IG Bio RTM, IG Inbox RTM, IG Bio Faris Root, IG Inbox Faris Root, IG Story Faris Root, YT Bio Faris Root, YT Bio RTM, IG Comment Rootmedia, Website RTM, Referral, Call and Walk-In. They are already included in the Metrix counts above.
  • The Meta and Metrix counts will not match exactly: one ad chat is not always entered in Metrix, and Metrix also holds leads that did not come from ads.
  • Weeks here are fixed blocks of the month: 1–7, 8–14, 15–21, 22–28 and 29 to month end.
